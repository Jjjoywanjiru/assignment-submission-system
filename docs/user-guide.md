# User Guide

## For students

### Register and log in
1. Open the site and choose **Register**.
2. Enter your name, email and a password, then submit.
3. Choose **Login** and sign in with your email and password.

### View assignments
After logging in, open **Assignments**. Each assignment shows its title,
deadline and whether you have already submitted.

### Submit an assignment
1. Open the assignment and choose **Submit**.
2. Pick your file. Allowed types: pdf, doc, docx, txt, zip, py.
3. Press **Submit**. If you submit after the deadline it is marked **Late**.

### Check your submission status
Open **My Submissions** to see every assignment you have submitted, the file
name, the time, and whether it was on time or late.

### Edit a submission before the deadline
Made a mistake? You can replace your file until the deadline.

1. Open **My Submissions** and choose **Edit** next to the submission
   (or go to `/submissions/<id>/edit`).
2. Choose the new file and press **Save changes**.
3. Your old file is replaced and the page shows when it was last edited.

Things to know:
- Once the deadline passes, the page shows that the submission is locked and
  you cannot change it.
- You can only edit your own submissions.
- An edit before the deadline does not make your work late.

## For lecturers

### Log in
Use the lecturer account provided to you and choose **Login**.

### Create an assignment
1. Open **Assignments** and choose **Create assignment**.
2. Enter a title, description and deadline, then save.

### View submissions
Open an assignment to see each student's submission, the time it was
submitted (and last edited), and whether it was late. Use **Download** to open
a student's file.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "That file type is not allowed" | Convert your work to pdf, doc, docx, txt, zip or py. |
| "The deadline has passed" | Editing is closed. Contact your lecturer. |
| Cannot log in | Check your email and password, or register first. |
